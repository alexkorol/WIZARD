# Archive policy

WIZARD keeps some historical experiments on disk so direct URLs and git history still work. They are not part of the Verdigris Systems Laboratory public surface.

## Rules

- Do not physically delete or relocate archive candidates unless a later task packet says so.
- Archive candidates must be absent from the dashboard, featured copy, social metadata, generated active registry, and active-module documentation.
- Direct URLs may keep working. Redirects are deferred.

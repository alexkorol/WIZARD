# Crafting and loot: provisional system

Status: provisional (2026-11-09). Agents built this to owner rule 52; the owner tunes it in playtest. Numbers are starting values, not decisions.

Owner, 2026-11-09: "for crafting mechanics we can sort of use the blueprint that PoE and PoE 2 laid out, adopt and adapt it to our ARPG without completely copying it completely … the brands and bonds instead of prefixes and suffixes. I need the ai codes to invent a temporary system for now and I will manually tune it as I playtest. Another big difference is how lootpools work and how items are tied to houses as relics and can be found out and about when a character permadies in battle. So no pre-made set or unique items, unless they emerged thru player character development and interaction".

## Item

- **Base:** form × material, with the base's implicit (PoE implicit).
- **Brands** take the place of PoE prefixes: fixed stat lines rolled from the base's brand pool, tiered by item level and material.
- **Bonds** take the place of PoE suffixes: stat lines that grow with the character's deeds (bond XP, Bond I → III).
- **Rarity** (PoE): Normal holds no modifiers; Magic holds one or two; Rare holds up to two brands and two bonds (four modifiers).

## Crafting (PoE currency, function-named)

| Currency | PoE analogue | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Add Modifier | Augmentation / Exalted Orb | Adds one modifier while the rarity has room |
| Upgrade to Rare | Regal Orb | Makes a Magic item Rare and adds one modifier |
| Reroll Values | Divine Orb | Rerolls modifier values within their tiers |
| Remove Last Modifier | Annulment Orb (adapted: last, not random) | Removes the most recently added modifier |
| Guaranteed X Brand, pigments | PoE2 Omens, Essences | Guarantee which brand the next craft adds |

Nothing else limits crafting: no per-item crafting budget, no material-upgrade gamble, no permanently locked slots, no class penalty on bonds.

## Special items

- No pre-made unique or set items drop or exist in loot tables.
- An item becomes special only through play: when its three bonds reach Bond III it becomes an upgraded item named after the character who raised it, with one theme power. No set bonuses.

## Loot and houses

- Items belong to the house. When a character dies in battle (permadeath), everything they carried stays at the death site as a recovery of the house; any later character of that house can find it there and take it back. There is no automatic inheritance.
- **Item Rarity** (PoE "increased Rarity of Items found") raises the chance that a dropped item is Magic or Rare.

## Removed from the earlier draft

Crafting uses (Patience), the material-upgrade gamble (kiln), locked slots (scars), half-strength bonds for another class (kinship), the set bonus for upgraded items, automatic inheritance (Lineage), and the pre-made Mana set and unique (ARPG `spiritwork-*` recipes).

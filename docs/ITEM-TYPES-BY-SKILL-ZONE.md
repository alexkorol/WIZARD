# Item types by skill zone

Version 2026-11-07.3. Generated by `tools/rpg_inventory/core/build_item_types_by_skill_zone.py`; edit the data there, not this file.
Machine-readable copy: `tools/rpg_inventory/core/item-types-by-skill-zone.json`. Visual: `tools/rpg_inventory/skill-zone-items.html` (PNG: `docs/visual/item-types-by-skill-zone.png`).

## Why

A brief like "DEX/INT helmet" asks the image model to invent a look for a stat pair, and it fills the gap with fantasy defaults. Instead, each zone of the passive tree is defined by the skills that live there; the item types are the tools a Bronze Age person doing those skills would carry and wear, each tied to a real find, depiction or text. If no evidence exists, the item is marked unconfirmed and not briefed (owner rule 51).

## Owner defaults (default, owner may veto)

Set 2026-11-07 from the Dispatch review. Each is a default the owner may veto; briefs and modpools follow them until then.

- Horned helmet: kept only as the STR/INT outer 'Bronze horned helmet', because it is attested (Veksø helmets c. 900 BC; Sherden reliefs c. 1175 BC). STR and other zones ban fantasy (Viking-style) horned helmets.
- Prefix/suffix: adopted. 2 prefixes + 2 suffixes inside the game's 4-mod limit, plus one implicit on weapon, off-hand and jewellery bases (none on body, head, hands, feet, belt). Needs a code change in Itemization.cs to take effect.
- Added Fire: limited to fire-themed bases (STR/INT and the fire and ignite wedge), as mapped. This changes live drops once implemented; today the game rolls it on every weapon.
- Mines: buried stake traps and laid sling shot are the period answer (Bundle of sharpened stakes, Pouch of clay sling shot). No fire pots.
- Blink, phase and shadow: the disguise kit (animal skin hood, wolf skin cloak) is the visual answer.
- Claws: keep the existing Bone Claws base; brief no new claw items.
- Jawbone weapon: retired from new briefs (no period find). The game base and action stay as they are.
- Period weighting: core leans Neolithic/Chalcolithic common kit; mid is Early/Middle Bronze Age; outer is Late Bronze Age elite pieces. Regions stay mixed.
- War Horn: goes in the War Horn slot (unlocked by the STR class node), not the off hand.
- Proposed mod numbers: first-pass values kept, marked provisional (status 'proposed', provisional: true).

## Ground truth used

- Passive tree: `the ARPG/Assets/ARPG/Resources/Wizard/tree-data.js.txt` (generator `tools/geometric_skilltree/scripts/author-tree.mjs`). Ten rings, one Origin, six spokes at 60 degree steps: INT 90, INT/DEX 150, DEX 210, DEX/STR 270, STR 330, STR/INT 30. Class node at ring 7 of each spoke; six themed wedges between spokes.
- Skills: fixed actions in `Core/ActionPresentation.cs`, 49 spells in `Core/SpellRules.cs`. No skill or item carries an attribute tag; zone membership is inferred from wedge themes, class nodes and the stated Weaving rows (`Core/Weaving.cs:115-142`: wand or knife -> Focus, rod or axe -> Alignment, staff or two-hander -> Sphere, spear -> Reach, shield or buckler -> Element).
- Shared chat "Compare PoE Skill Types": 303 archetypes with INT/DEX/STR weights. Its files were not retrievable; its published sample placements are used as a secondary guide. Where the chat and the game disagree (summoning: chat INT, game: the companion and Ward wedge toward STR/INT), the game wins.
- Item pipeline: `tools/rpg_inventory/core/ANCIENT-EQUIPMENT-TAXONOMY.md` (A0-A2 rows only), `GENERATION-PLAN.md` and `EXPANSION-CORRECTIONS-2026-07-24.md` slop lists, owner rules 6-12 and 51.

## Ring bands

| Band | Rings | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| core | 1-2 | Shared mechanics near the Origin. Everyday kit any build can wear; cheapest materials (flint, bone, hide, wool, linen). Period: leans Neolithic and Chalcolithic common kit. |
| mid | 3-6 | Versatile and hybrid archetypes. The working tools of the trade (copper, early bronze, specialist organic kit). Period: Early and Middle Bronze Age. |
| outer | 7-10 | Specialised archetypes past the ring-7 class node. Full professional or high-status kit (bronze, gold, ivory, inlay); matches the class unlocks. Period: Late Bronze Age elite pieces. |

Confidence codes: **A** attested: real find of this form in the period; **Art** attested in period art or texts only; construction inferred; **Adapt** attested elsewhere or for another use; adapted here (disclose); **Disp** object attested, but the function used here is disputed (disclose); **U** unconfirmed: do not generate without owner sign-off.

## Zones

### INT (90 degrees)

- **Tree:** ring-7 INT node: sets the INT class; unlocks a second curio, a spell focus socket and an extra focus slot. Wedges: cold and marks wedge (INT toward INT/DEX): cold, chill, freeze, marks; INT spoke: Ward, spell damage. Weaving: damage type row (opened by the INT class).
- **Game skills here (inferred zone):** frost-nova, blizzard, frozen-orb, geyser, holy-freeze (aura), arcane-ward, energy-shield, teleport, cursed (curse), weakened (curse), power-charges (inferred).
- **Archetypes (chat INT/DEX/STR):** spell bolt 95/4/1, nova, curse 90/8/2, channelled spells, teleport 73/27/0, ward.
- **Who does this in the period:** Ritual specialist who is also healer and diviner, not a battle-mage. Evidence: shaman burials (Hilazon Tachtit c. 10,000 BC; Bad Duerrenberg c. 7000 BC; Upton Lovell G2a c. 1800 BC), Mari liver models (18th c. BC), the Ramesseum magician's box (c. 1700 BC).
- **Core kit:** Wooden staff, Clay drum, Felt cap, Oval wool cloak, Spiral arm rings, Calfskin belt with pouch, Bearskin-soled shoes, Drilled tooth pendant, Gold wire spiral ring.
- **Mid kit:** Bronze serpent wand, Clay liver model, Leather charm bag on a belt, Rush sandals, Faience eye amulet.
- **Outer kit:** Ivory curved wand, Bronze sistrum, Antler headdress, Sheet gold cone hat, Hide garment with bone point fringe, Leopard skin over linen robe, Linen gloves, Stone cylinder seal on a cord, Scarab seal ring.
- **Do not make in this zone:** gem-tipped or glowing staffs; robes with sigils, star patterns or trim; floppy hats; books, scrolls, candles.
- **Open questions:**
  - No wand-like object is attested outside Egypt; INT wands follow Egyptian finds only.
  - No wooden frame drum survives; the clay drum stands in. Is a hide frame drum acceptable as adapted?
  - Hvidegaard bag contents need checking against the primary publication before a brief uses them.
  - Default, owner may veto (2026-11-07): period weighting by band: core leans Neolithic/Chalcolithic common kit, mid Early/Middle Bronze Age, outer Late Bronze Age elite pieces. Regions stay mixed.

### INT/DEX (150 degrees)

- **Tree:** ring-7 INT/DEX node: sets the INT/DEX class; unlocks trap and mark tools, poison vials and a supply pack. Wedges: poison and traps wedge (DEX toward INT/DEX): poison, wither, traps, stealth; cold and marks wedge: marks. Weaving: effect row (opened by the INT/DEX class, a wand or a knife).
- **Game skills here (inferred zone):** poison-cloud, grasping-vines, drain-life, malevolence (aura), withered (curse), blinded (curse), chain-lightning, spark, shock-nova, tornado (placement weakest).
- **Archetypes (chat INT/DEX/STR):** blink 64/34/2, mark 53/45/2, shadow clone 49/49/2, mine 45/52/3, trap 36/61/3, poison, stealth.
- **Who does this in the period:** Hunter-trapper and poisoner, and the night scout. Evidence: Oetzi's bast net and kit (c. 3300 BC); Egyptian clap-net fowling scenes; Border Cave and Kruger Cave poison applicators; boar's-tusk helmet of the scout. Blink, phase and shadow clone have no physical analogue; the nearest real kit is disguise (animal skins) and pigment for marks.
- **Core kit:** Flint dagger with bast sheath, Bearskin cap with chin strap, Hide strip coat, Belt pouch with fire kit, Bearskin-soled shoes, Pierced tooth pendant, Plain copper wire ring.
- **Mid kit:** Bone knife, Bronze serpent wand, Bast fowling net, Pigment palette with kohl tube, Grass cape, Stone wrist guard, Esparto grass bag, Esparto grass sandals, Ochre crayon on a thong, Bundle of sharpened stakes, Pouch of clay sling shot.
- **Outer kit:** Horn poison container with applicator, Animal skin hood with head, Wolf skin cloak.
- **Do not make in this zone:** clay fire pots or bombs as mines (no pre-Iron-Age evidence); caltrops; black hoods with shadowed faces; glowing poison vials.
- **Open questions:**
  - Default, owner may veto (2026-11-07): mines are buried stake traps and laid sling shot. Clay fire pots stay out (not attested before the Iron Age). The stake bundle stays unconfirmed (not briefable) until a stake-lined pit find is cited; pit traps themselves are attested.
  - Default, owner may veto (2026-11-07): blink, phase and shadow clone use the disguise kit (animal skin hood, wolf skin cloak) as their visual answer.
  - Bronze Age arrow poison in Eurasia is not proven archaeologically; the poison container rests on southern African evidence.
  - Lightning spells sit here only by inference (shock support is DEX, scaling is INT). Confirm the zone before briefing their foci.

- **Who does this in the period:** Hunter-archer and light missile troop: Oetzi's bow and quiver (c. 3300 BC), Beaker archer burials (Amesbury Archer c. 2300 BC), Egyptian archers and slingers (Tutankhamun's bows, slings and throw-sticks c. 1325 BC; Mesehti archer models c. 2000 BC). Dash and evasion fit a skirmisher in clothing, not plates.

- **Do not make in this zone:** fire pots or grenades (use sling and clay shot); studded leather 'rogue' armour; ranger boots; hooded archers with shadowed faces.
- **Open questions:**
  - Default, owner may veto (2026-11-07): claws keep the existing Bone Claws game base, but no new claw items are briefed (no period precedent found).
  - Javelin versus thrusting spear: no clear Bronze Age find-type split; lighter heads are an assumption.

- **Open questions:**
  - Default, owner may veto (2026-11-07): the war horn goes in the War Horn slot, not the off hand (horns and trumpets are attested: Tutankhamun's trumpets, lurs, animal horns).
  - Default, owner may veto (2026-11-07): the Jawbone weapon is retired from new briefs (no period find; the Samson story is a later text). The existing game base and its Jawbone combo action are untouched.

  - Banded stone maul needs a specific drilled-stone hammer find as its shape reference before briefing.

- **Mid kit:** Copper sceptre mace, Fossil stone mace with bone mounts, Figure-of-eight shield, Bronze helmet with plume tube, Leather scale corselet, Gold armlets, Leather belt with bronze disc, Carnelian and lapis bead collar.

- **Open questions:**

  - The pipeline's pack names are now plain (relic pack, supply pack, animal parts pack); see owner-rules/rename-map.json.
  - Summoning: the game puts companions in the companion and Ward wedge (INT toward STR/INT), while the shared chat put summoning in pure INT. This doc follows the game.
  - Reed buckler is a game base with no confirmed find; check before briefing new reed shields.

## Modpools

Each base rolls the mods that support the job its zone's skills do, plus a small shared pool. Same zone and skill keys as the item tables above; data in `item_modpools.py`, output under each item's `modpool` in the JSON.

- **source:** Assets/ARPG/Scripts/Core/Itemization.cs:115-128 (live pool) and WizardCombat.cs (stat consumers). Live values are copied, never changed here.
- **jobRule:** A base rolls mods whose tags match the skills it serves (job, full weight), defence mods its material implies (material, full weight: metal and tusk -> Guard; hide, leather, fur, grass -> Evasion; linen, wool, felt, gold, ivory, faience -> Ward, after the PoE cloth convention), and its zone's themes (zone, 0.6 weight), plus a small shared pool (life, resistances, the zone's own attributes, and flask mods on belts; 0.5 weight). Everything else is excluded with a reason.
- **affixCap:** Default, owner may veto: 2 prefixes + 2 suffixes, inside the game's MaxModifiers 4. The game has no prefix/suffix split yet (code change needed).
- **tiers:** Tiers unlock by item level as in the game (typically ilvl 1 / 20-30 / 50-60); tier pick weights 10/5/2 (Itemization.TierWeights). A base's band sets its minimum item level: core 1, mid 15, outer 45.
- **weights:** Mod weight = catalogue weight x role factor (job 1.0, zone 0.6, shared 0.5), so job mods dominate.
- **implicits:** Default, owner may veto: one fixed implicit per weapon, off-hand and jewellery base, taken from the base's form (spear: reach; tower shield: block and Guard). Body armour, helmets, gloves and boots carry no implicit; their base defence is the material.
- **addedFire:** Default, owner may veto: added-fire rolls only on fire-tagged bases (STR/INT and the fire and ignite wedge); the live game rolls it on every weapon until Itemization.cs changes.
- **status:** `live` in the roll pool today; `consumer` combat already reads the stat; add the mod to the pool; `proposed` needs a new combat consumer before it can roll; values are provisional first-pass numbers.

Example: the Bast fowling net (INT/DEX off-hand tool; serves traps) rolls ailment-effect, intelligence, curse-effect, mark-effect, trap-damage, trap-arming, trap-count, poison-chance. It cannot roll spirited, warded, rite-damage, rite-speed, elemental-damage, cooldown, ignite-chance, chill-effect, area, warcry-effect, aura-effect, companion-damage, companion-life, channel-cost: none of its skills are attacks, spells or auras.

### Bases with thin or one-sided pools (29)

Weapons and off-hands are thin with fewer than 3 job or zone mods. Armour, belts and jewellery count their material defences too, but need at least one skill-linked mod. Either is also flagged when every job mod still needs a combat consumer. Most thin bases serve only "any": they are generic kit and either need a job (a skill they help) or should stay low-value core bases.

Causes, in order of frequency: (1) generic kit whose only listed job is "any" (plain belts, rings, caps, tunics); give it a skill it helps or keep it as a low-value core base. (2) Slot types with few catalogue mods: quivers and bow cases have only projectile, accuracy, thrown and Dexterity, because arrow count, pierce and similar mods have no combat consumer; shields have only block, Guard and stun. (3) Off-zone materials: a leather belt in a STR zone gets Evasion from its material but nothing from the zone.

## Do not make (all zones)

- iron or steel look (grey sheen, fullers, crossguards); no chainmail, rivet-mail or plate armour
- stitched modern soles, welts, heels, toe caps on any footwear
- aprons, kilts, loincloths or hanging panels presented as body armour; belts drawn as faulds or tassets
- forked or gnarled sticks as staffs or weapons; crystal-topped staffs
- stone ball set in a metal cup as a 'stone mace' (real maceheads are drilled through and sit on a wooden haft)
- invented-name items, lore epithets or compound coinages (owner rules 6-8)
- medieval silhouettes: closed, crested, visored or nasal helmets; heater or kite shields; longswords; buckles with tongues
- glass vials, potion bottles, lanterns, candles, coins, books, scrolls
- glowing gems, energy veins, glow halos, floating crystals
- pentagrams, rune bands, sigils, pseudo-text or pseudo-cuneiform
- single boot or glove where a pair is the item; cropped cords or shafts; arrows shown in quivers (pipeline rule)

## How the pipeline uses this

1. Pick a zone and band (not a stat pair). The zone's skills say what the character does.
2. Take the slot items for that zone and band from the JSON; skip any with `briefable: false`.
3. The brief names the item, its construction, silhouette and precedent, and quotes the item's `avoid` plus `globalAvoid`. Supply a shape reference (artefact image) where the noun's usual picture is wrong (rule 51).
4. A coherent loadout call (RUNBOOK addendum) uses one faction + zone + band in place of faction + axis + tier.
5. Mods for a base come from its `modpool`; never add a mod to a base whose skills it does not serve.

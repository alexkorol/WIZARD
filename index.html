Featured Work

Vessels of Life & Mana

A real-time WebGL2 health and mana orb engine, rendered by a single fullscreen fragment shader. Live procedural liquids — ink-plume blood and a condensing malachite nebula — are composited into AI-generated art plates with dynamic statue relighting. A status simulation drives poison, bleed, and mana reservation as you watch.

# Glossary (closed)

Every capitalised word in player-facing text, and every word of a Title Case label or stat name, in WIZARD and the ARPG must be one of these terms. `owner-rules/lint.mjs` fails otherwise, in both repositories' gates. Source: glossary.json, generated by term-inventory.mjs from term-verdicts.json.

Rule: Keep a term only if (a) the owner introduced it, (b) it is a plain English description of what it does, or (c) it is the standard PoE1/PoE2 term used in the same meaning. Everything else is purged (renamed plainly, or removed with its mechanic if that mechanic was invented and does nothing). 'Spirit' and 'blood' were named by the owner as made up (2026-11-08).

Terms: 3315 (owner 53, PoE 174, plain 2855, other kept 233).

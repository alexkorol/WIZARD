THE MASON

Border tile forge: complete autotile transition sets for where two terrains meet. Square blob and hex, carved from a seed.

Paint with the mouse — left adds the inner terrain, Shift or right-drag erases. Two AI routes: import seamless textures (from gpt-image-2 or any model) and the Mason cuts the transitions; or export the template sheet, paint over it, and import the painted sheet back — the playground then autotiles with your art.

# Faction Historical-Construction Moodboards v1

**Superseded for generation use.** These archive-heavy boards remain only as
an audit trail. Use `../faction-moodboards-v2/` and
`../../core/MIXED-REFERENCE-BOARD-POLICY.md` for current wildcard generation.

These boards are supplemental plausibility references for the manual web
wildcard workflow. They are not design-authority sheets or material shopping
lists.

For each generation request, attach:

1. The two reviewed character-ladder references for one faction, axis, and tier.
2. Exactly one matching faction moodboard from this folder.
3. The matching paperdoll wildcard prompt.

The character references remain the authority for the coherent loadout,
silhouette language, tier, and which items belong together. Use the moodboard
only to ground construction, proportions, joins, fastening, layering, and
material behavior. Never mix faction boards in one request.

The boards use reviewed museum objects, museum reconstructions, and explicitly
marked later continuity or construction-comparator examples. Every tile states
the narrow feature that may be transferred. Source URLs, evidence type, and
prepared source paths are recorded in `manifest.json`.

## Global exclusions

- Do not copy corrosion, excavation damage, museum mounts, lighting, or backgrounds.
- Do not copy sacred or culture-specific imagery, spiral motifs, readable text, or symbols.
- Do not infer that every material on a board belongs on every item.
- Do not invent unsupported paperdoll slots to fill a batch.
- Quivers are open and empty; arrows are separate inventory items.

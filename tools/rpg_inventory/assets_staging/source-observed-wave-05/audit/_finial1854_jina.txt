Description
Copper alloy 'master of animals' standard-finial; produced as a single lost-wax casting; represents a Janus figure with prominent nose, eyes, eyebrows and mouth, and long ears, grappling with a pair of stylised lions in typical Luristan style; hollow tube with traces of what may be the original contents in situ.

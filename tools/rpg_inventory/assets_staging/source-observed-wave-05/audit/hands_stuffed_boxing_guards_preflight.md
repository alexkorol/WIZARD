# Wave 05 preflight — `hands_stuffed_boxing_guards`

Verdict: **REJECT / retire current paired target**

No prompt was written and no art was generated.

## Decision

The Vindolanda finds are excellent direct evidence for a Roman leather knuckle
guard, but they do not support the current target:

> A pair of empty rounded rawhide boxing guards enclosing palm, thumb base, and
> knuckles as continuous stuffed hand cages, with thick impact rolls and no hard
> inserts.

Two guards survive, but authoritative reporting explicitly says they are not a
matching pair. The smaller and larger objects differ in dimensions, padding,
wear, repair history, edge treatment, and likely use. Neither is a full glove:
each is a padded leather band or pouch laid transversely over the knuckles, with
the hand passing through a large oval opening. Fingers, palm, and thumb remain
substantially exposed. There is no separate thumb volume or continuous hand
cage.

Producing the required pair would therefore require either:

1. mirroring one surviving guard to invent its counterpart; or
2. falsely presenting the two unlike practice/fighting guards as a matched
   left/right set.

Both routes fail the pair and structural-counterpart gates. The target's
enclosed palm/thumb geometry independently contradicts the object.

## Observed construction locks

These locks describe the source and must not be converted into a generation
brief while the target is rejected.

- Read as a broad oval leather band with one thick transverse knuckle roll and a
  large central hand opening.
- One leather piece folded into a pouch/roll; side extensions or ends meet to
  close the oval.
- The hand opening is structural negative space, not a palm cutout in a mitten.
- No full-hand body, enclosed palm, backhand shell, finger stalls, separate thumb
  volume, wrist cuff, or forearm extension.
- Smaller guard: softer, layered interwoven or tightly coiled twisted-leather
  padding; visible knuckle impression.
- Larger guard: straw/natural stuffing, hardened leather edge strip, heavy
  contact wear, and a circular repair patch over a tear.
- Preserve the two constructions as different objects; do not average them into
  one design and repeat it.
- No modern boxing-glove bulb, wrist laces, eyelets, hook-and-loop strap, padded
  thumb, palm mesh, brand mark, or anatomical shaping.
- No metal inserts, studs, spikes, blades, knuckle plates, finger plates, or
  floating straps.

## Pair and structural-counterpart ruling

The runtime glove slot normally requires both pieces in one icon. A valid pair
must be either:

- a documented matched pair; or
- two source-supported structural counterparts whose individual differences and
  left/right logic are visible and credible.

Vindolanda supplies neither. It supplies one smaller soft practice guard and one
larger, harder, heavily used/repaired guard. The sources do not assign them as
left and right. Treating them as a pair would be a museum-display grouping, not
a wearable pair.

The pair must not be solved by:

- horizontal reflection;
- copying the less-damaged guard twice;
- copying the larger repaired guard twice;
- placing the two unlike guards together and calling their differences
  left/right asymmetry;
- adding a thumb pocket or palm cage to make them look handed;
- adding straps to imply a missing counterpart.

## S 2×2 framing ruling

The target's canonical canvas remains **S 2×2**, but no generation may proceed.

If a future exact matched pair or credible full reconstruction clears the gate,
the square composition should show both empty guards at complementary
three-quarter angles, slightly overlapping but with both oval hand openings,
knuckle rolls, joined ends, and underside construction readable. No hands,
forearms, mannequin parts, or ghost anatomy may hold the shapes open.

The pair must fill the square without becoming two modern boxing gloves or two
flat bracelet-like rings.

## Active-service finish

The archaeological photographs show waterlogged, conserved, collapsed,
darkened, cracked objects. Those preservation effects are evidence for
construction but not the intended game-item finish.

A source-cleared reconstruction would require:

- conditioned, serviceable dark-brown leather;
- stuffed rolls retaining functional volume;
- softened edge wear and hand-shaped creasing;
- the larger object's circular repair patch and contact wear only if rendering
  that exact type;
- readable seams/joins without copying every tear, hole, crack, black
  conservation sheen, collapsed fold, display wire, or museum mount.

Active-service restoration must not invent missing closures, straps, thumb
pieces, laces, or metal.

## Hazards

- Generating modern bulbous boxing gloves.
- Adding wrist laces, eyelets, cuffs, or closure straps.
- Inventing a thumb pocket, thumb hood, enclosed palm, fingers, or backhand.
- Mirroring one archaeological object.
- Misrepresenting the unlike surviving guards as left/right mates.
- Combining the small guard's leather coil with the large guard's patch,
  hardened edge, and straw stuffing into a repeated hybrid.
- Adding metal because later Roman cesti are sometimes described with metal.
- Turning the hardened leather edge into a blade or metal ridge.
- Rendering hands, skin, fingers, wrists, arms, mannequin forms, or ghost limbs.
- Copying black conservation sheen, desiccation cracks, torn gaps, collapsed
  stuffing, museum supports, or display lighting as active-service design.
- Flattening the object into a wrist ring, belt, or bracelet.
- Extending it into a bracer or full mitten to satisfy familiar model priors.
- Adding unattached or floating straps.

## Disposition

Retire `hands_stuffed_boxing_guards` in its current paired, enclosed-hand-cage
form. Do not prompt or generate it.

A future roster proposal could use one source-faithful **single Roman knuckle
guard** only if the runtime and user approve an explicitly asymmetric single
handwear item. A paired replacement requires a documented matched pair or a
credible reconstruction that independently establishes both non-mirrored
counterparts. Neither recovery is authorized by this audit.

## Why this candidate fails

The target is a prose invention rather than a transcription of an inspected complete object. British Museum 1913,1108.28 is only an 8.9 cm perforated copper bird-beak fitting from the tip of a combined gorytus. The two exact official photographs show no complete container and no organic construction.

The museum source therefore cannot support the target’s bundled reed body, dark mineral lacquer, fitted hardwood mouth hoop, wooden base, leather back strip, compact strap, fiber/sinew joins, or flush wooden lid. Removing the unsupported copper does not turn the source into evidence for those organic features.

The flush lid is independently disqualifying. The user’s current quiver rule requires a visibly open, empty mouth with no visible arrows. A closed or flush lid hides the required empty state.

The concept is also redundant. The current roster already has an accepted open empty rawhide tube and several reviewed open empty slatted/organic container images. A newly generated plain cylindrical reed tube would not add a sufficiently distinct macro-silhouette.

This confirms the durable retirement already recorded in `core/EXPANSION-CORRECTIONS-2026-07-24.md` and `core/PILOT-02-REPORT.md`.

## Hard locks for any future quiver work

- Complete standalone quiver container.
- Mouth visibly open and visibly empty.
- No arrows, shafts, fletching, bow, bow limb, bowstring, grip, or combined archery set.
- No lid, cap, or flush closure under the current user rule.
- Reed, bast, common wood, plain hide, and ordinary cloth remain organic-led.
- No bronze/brass collars, rim bands, base cups, rivets, buckles, knobs, or polished prestige trim unless an exact complete-object source proves the metal as structural and the item’s material thesis allows it.
- Do not reconstruct a full container from a fragment alone.
- Do not rename woven textile, hide, or wood slats as reed.
- Do not infer lacquer from a dark surface.
- Do not turn a combined gorytus fitting into a generic arrow-only cylindrical quiver.
- Do not generate every quiver visible on a mock character board.

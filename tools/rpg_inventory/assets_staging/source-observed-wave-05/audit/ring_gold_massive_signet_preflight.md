# Wave05 preflight — `ring_gold_massive_signet`

## Verdict

**REJECT the current row/source pairing. Do not generate.**

BM EA68868 is a legitimate, complete, massive gold signet and its geometry is
excellent for a 1x1 ARPG ring. The current target nevertheless fails two
decisive gates:

1. Its blank face is not supported by the exact object. The incised
   hieroglyphic name and title are the functional sealing face and a defining
   part of the museum identification. Removing them produces an invented
   uninscribed version; copying or approximating them would violate the
   project's symbol restrictions.
2. `ring_skymetal_signet` reuses this exact hollowed lozenge geometry for
   another 1x1 signet. Gold versus dark metal is not enough structural
   separation, especially beside the accepted and promoted bronze signets.

The massive-gold-signature family is roster-worthy in principle, but it needs
a different exact blank/abstract-faced source and must not coexist with a
same-shape skymetal signet.

The official record establishes:

- Gold signet-ring of Sheshonq.
- Egyptian 26th Dynasty, **circa 575 BCE**.
- Gold; mould-made and incised.
- Diameter 3 cm; height 3.5 cm; length 2.4 cm; weight 82 g.
- Massive Late Period form with a very thick lozenge-shaped bezel.
- The back of the bezel was cut away to accommodate the wearer's finger.
- Shank and bezel were made in **separate moulds and joined together**.
- The bezel is incised in hieroglyphic script with Sheshonq's name and title,
  “Chief Steward of the divine adoratrice.”
- The face could be pressed into mud to seal documents and objects.
- Condition: good.

## Blank-face finding

**A blank face is not supported by EA68868.**

The face is not incidental ornament that can be erased while preserving the
object's source identity. It carries the owner's name and office and is the
working die for sealing. The large incised field is also what visually fills
the massive bezel.

The available safe choices are both unacceptable for this exact row:

- Copying the hieroglyphs would reproduce a real personal/title inscription
  and violate the no-hieroglyph rule.
- Replacing them with pseudo-script, generic glyphs, a sun, or another emblem
  invents unsupported content.
- Leaving the entire face blank invents an uninscribed object and removes the
  defining functional surface treatment.

Therefore this source cannot simultaneously satisfy exact-source fidelity and
the symbol restriction.

## Construction locks if a different blank-faced source is found

- Complete heavy gold hoop with broad, smoothly swelling shoulders.
- Thick rounded-lozenge bezel, not circular, oval, octagonal, rectangular, or
  gem-set.
- Deep finger-clearance cutaway behind the thick bezel must remain visibly
  plausible.
- Separate shank and bezel made in separate moulds and joined. Do not invent
  rivets, solder blobs, collars, plate straps, or a false exposed seam.
- Broad continuous gold mass; no gemstone, colored inlay, separate face plate,
  hollow modern casting shell, or thin decorative filigree.
- Active-service gold: warm dense metal, satin handling polish, shallow
  scratches, softened contact edges, and minor irregularity. No mirror-modern
  polish, chrome reflections, burial crust, heavy blackening, or fake cracks.
- No hieroglyphs, pseudo-script, sun/radial motif, royal cartouche, animal
  figure, portrait, gem, or invented sacred mark.

These are salvage locks for a new source, not authorization to generate from
EA68868.

## S 1x1 ARPG framing

The ring is physically massive for a finger ring—3 cm diameter, 3.5 cm high,
and 82 g—and can read clearly in S 1x1 if framed tightly.

A slightly elevated three-quarter angle should show:

- the lozenge face;
- bezel thickness;
- both shoulders;
- the complete hoop;
- a dark interior opening;
- the underside/finger cutaway as a secondary depth cue.

Do not crop the lower hoop or flatten the ring face-on. A face-on view hides
the separate-body construction and cutaway; a pure side view loses the
lozenge identity.

## Failure taxonomy

- **Exact-source contradiction:** blank face versus defining incised
  inscription.
- **Symbol bind:** copying real hieroglyphs is forbidden; pseudo-script or
  replacement emblems are invented.
- **Direct planned duplicate:** same source and undercut-lozenge geometry as
  `ring_skymetal_signet`.
- **Existing signet crowding:** promoted octagonal bronze and round dark
  signets already occupy blank/abstract authority-face reads.
- **Join invention:** false rivets, solder lumps, collars, face plates, or
  exposed seams added to explain the separate moulds.
- **Underside omission:** solid thick bezel blocks the finger opening or loses
  the documented cutaway.
- **Integral-construction drift:** hoop and bezel become one smooth modern
  casting without believable shoulder/join logic.
- **Modern jewelry drift:** mirror polish, gem setting, machined symmetry,
  thin showroom band, or contemporary cushion-signature styling.
- **Symbol fallback:** sun, starburst, cartouche, animal, portrait,
  pseudo-hieroglyphs, or generic fantasy rune.
- **Framing failure:** face-on token, cropped hoop, or tiny ring that does not
  expose the massive construction.

## Disposition

Remove or replace the present `ring_gold_massive_signet` source/brief pairing.
If the gold massive signet is retained as a roster goal, find a dated complete
object with a genuinely blank or safely abstract face and retire/reconceptualize
`ring_skymetal_signet` so the two do not share one undercut-lozenge base.

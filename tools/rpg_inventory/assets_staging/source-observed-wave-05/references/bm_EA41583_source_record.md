## Published technical evidence

Cartwright and Taylor’s table of 15 Egyptian self bows lists EA41583 as a Middle Kingdom Beni Hasan specimen and identifies its wood as Acacia sp. The paper’s catalogue text describes it as a wooden bow in good condition, 174.3 cm long, excavated by John Garstang at Beni Hasan; the exact findspot and exact date were not recorded, with Middle Kingdom given as the probable date.

A later Liverpool doctoral study on ancient Egyptian bows reports that EA41583 has evidence at one tip of both sinew wrapping and a groove made as a nock. The same study says no decurved bow in its corpus retains an original string. This construction evidence is textual and must not be extrapolated beyond that one tip.

# British Museum 1986,0705.1 catalogue snapshot

Retrieved 2026-07-25 from:
<https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/H_1986-0705-1>

- Object types: censer; chain
- Museum and registration number: 1986,0705.1
- Culture/period: Early Byzantine
- Production date: 6th–7th century
- Findspot: Glastonbury Abbey, abbey trench
- Material: copper alloy
- Techniques: twisted; drawn
- Dimensions: bowl height 72 mm; bowl width 88 mm; chain length 360 mm
- Condition: an old break crosses the shoulder to the right of one lug; a
  corresponding break crosses the rim to the left of the same lug
- Department: Britain, Europe and Prehistory
- Additional ID: codex ID 62694

Catalogue construction description:

- open-bowl type;
- three sturdy feet;
- hemispherical body;
- body constricts before flaring to a broad inverted shoulder;
- narrow in-turned rim;
- crudely drawn horizontal-line decoration;
- black deposit inside;
- one copper-alloy chain attached to two lugs;
- twisted figure-of-eight link at each attachment;
- larger finer-gauge terminal links;
- remaining chain made from twisted figure-of-eight links.

Residue analysis:

- the dark burnt interior deposit contains ash, metal corrosion products, and
  traces of gum resin;
- the catalogue interprets the resin as likely incense material such as myrrh
  or olibanum.

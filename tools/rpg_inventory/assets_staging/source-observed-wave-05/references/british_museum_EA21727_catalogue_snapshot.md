# British Museum EA21727 catalogue snapshot

Retrieved 2026-07-25 from:
<https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA21727>

- Object type: shoe
- Museum number / BM big number: EA21727
- Registration number: 1888,0514.15
- Description: right-foot shoe; leather lined with fur; sole and upper; upper
  embossed with a design of squares and lines; upper detached and broken into
  several pieces
- Culture/period: Graeco-Roman (?)
- Findspot: Akhmim
- Materials: leather; fur
- Technique: embossed
- Dimensions: height 1.40 cm; length 18 cm; width 8.10 cm; weight 33 g
- Condition: fair; incomplete and fragmentary
- Location: not on display
- Acquisition: donated by Rev. Greville John Chester, 1888
- Department: Egypt and Sudan

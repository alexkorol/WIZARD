## Prompt 1 - Four Factions, Tier 2 Warrior Source Pairs, Separate Images

```text
Generate a set of four separate images, no commentary.

TITLE / PURPOSE BLOCK
Photorealistic Bronze-Age ARPG Source Pair Calibration: Four Factions, Tier 2 Warriors

Create four separate polished character-reference images. Each image contains exactly two full-body adult fantasy Warriors standing side by side: one female-presenting Warrior and one male-presenting Warrior from the same faction, same class, and same gear tier. These are source images for later extraction into ARPG inventory item icons. They are not UI screens, not paperdoll layouts, not labeled sheets, and not story scenes.

GLOBAL STYLE BLOCK
Use cinematic, ultrafine-detail photorealistic 3D game render quality with crisp AAA asset clarity. The characters and gear should have sharp hard edges, readable seams, physical material response, high local contrast, and no painterly brush texture. Use strong upper-left studio key light, deep neutral shadows, and a cool rim light on armor, weapons, hair, cloth, and silhouettes. Keep color grading neutral-to-cold, with warm tones only where the material itself is warm: bronze, copper, hide, wool, shell, amber, or gold. No sepia haze, yellow wash, bloom fog, vignette, modern fashion lighting, or loose concept-art softness.

CANVAS AND COMPOSITION BLOCK
Each image uses a stark white or very pale blue-gray studio background only. No scenery, props behind the characters, text, labels, borders, UI, frames, floor emphasis, or decorative symbols. Each pair must be full-body and uncropped. Leave clear spacing between the two characters. Major gear must be visible: helmet or headgear, amulet or pendant, body armor, cloak or mantle, belt, hand protection, footwear, main hand weapon, and off hand shield or secondary weapon.

UNISEX EQUIPMENT BLOCK
All equipment bases are unisex. Do not create revealing female armor and covered male armor as separate categories. The female-presenting character may have armor fitted to her body, with a more shaped waist or feminine contour. The male-presenting character may be broader and heavier. But both use the same item-base language: same coverage level, same protection level, same construction logic, same material system, and same slot identity. A cuirass remains a cuirass, a mantle remains a mantle, a belt remains a horizontal belt, and a shield remains the same shield family across the pair.

CLASS AND TIER BLOCK
Both characters in every image are Tier 2 Warriors. Tier 2 means capable house-warrior gear: competent, desirable, well-made, and battlefield-ready, but not legendary, royal, supernatural, or boss-tier. Warrior gear should read as strength-focused and protective: broad silhouettes, structured torso armor, thick belts, bracers, greaves, heavy footwear, helmets or caps, shields, and forceful melee weapons. Do not make them rogues, mages, hunters, druids, performers, priests, dancers, or scouts.

SIDE-LAID ITEM REFERENCE BLOCK
In every image, include one major weapon or shield laid beside the pair as a clean reference object, fully visible and not embedded in the floor. This side-laid object must be complete, uncropped, and readable for later item extraction. Treat it as a real object next to the characters, not a UI icon. If it is a long weapon, show it tip-to-butt with shaft at least five times the length of the head. If it is a shield, show the clean front fighting face only.

FACTION 1: NORTHERN MARSH-TAIGA HOUSES
Cold river and forest-marsh house culture: birch, pine, peat bogs, dark wool, boiled leather, rawhide stitching, amber trade, bone toggles, carved dark wood, bronze plates, copper repairs, and practical wetland armor. Avoid medieval Vikings, horned helmets, chainmail, iron plate, fur-bikini cliches, runic overload, or generic barbarian styling. Use a bronze axe, bossed rawhide shield, heavy leather war cap, wool mantle, and sturdy hide boots.

FACTION 2: RED MESA-RAINFOREST CONFEDERACY
Pre-AD clay mesa and rainforest warrior culture with red mineral pigment, obsidian, shell, cotton armor, hardwood, copper, bone, turquoise or greenstone used sparingly, and layered woven protection. Blend northern and southern indigenous American inspiration without copying exact ceremonial dress. Avoid carnival feather clutter, giant headdresses, modern tribal costume, dangling charms everywhere, or weak wicker items. Use an obsidian-edged club, compact hardwood shield, quilted cotton cuirass, shell-edged bracers, and woven leg guards.

FACTION 3: STEPPE-SAND ROAD HOUSES
Nomadic trade-road and highland desert culture: felt, leather, lamellar plates, bronze fittings, horsehair cord, dense woven belts, small lapis or glass accents, weathered cotton, and practical travel armor. Blend steppe, Tibetan, Caucasus, Persian, and desert-nomad adjacent design cues without making it medieval, Ottoman, sci-fi, or belly-dancer costume. Use a heavy bronze saber-like chopping blade, reinforced round shield, felt-lined helmet, layered leather lamellar, and wrapped boots.

FACTION 4: NILE-ZIGGURAT HORN PALACES
Ancient river-palace and ziggurat city culture with darker warm skin tones, bronze, copper, bitumen-black leather, lapis-blue cloth, ivory or bone inlay, linen, hammered chest plates, and geometric palace craft. Blend Egyptian, Babylonian, Coptic, and Ethiopian-adjacent cues without copying sacred symbols. Avoid sun disks, human-face centerpieces, eight-spoked wheel motifs, readable writing, pharaoh costume cliches, or gold overload. Use a heavy bronze sickle blade or mace, tall bronze-faced shield, linen-backed armor, and dense leather sandals.

ANTI-CLUTTER AND NEGATIVE BLOCK
No high heels, no modern shoes, no zippers, no plastic, no guns, no sci-fi tech, no steel plate, no chainmail, no riveted mail, no medieval knight armor, no horned helmets, no glowing runes, no magical auras. Avoid dangling charms, bells, chimes, loose coins, delicate jewelry chains, and random hanging rings. Rings are compact finger jewelry only. Amulets are pendants on cords, not gorgets or collars. Belts are horizontal waist gear, not skirts or tassets. Shields show front fighting faces only, no front-side straps or dangling hardware.
```

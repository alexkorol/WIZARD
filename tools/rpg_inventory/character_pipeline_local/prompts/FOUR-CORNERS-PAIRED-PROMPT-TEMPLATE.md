Male style rule:
The male characters are masculine, powerful, and not lacking coverage. Do not make them default shirtless barbarians. Use fuller chest coverage, broader belts, stronger bracers, fuller greaves or leg wraps, more protective shoulders, and practical boots/sandals/wraps depending on faction. Construction and wearability:

Bone, shell, jade, copper, bronze, leather, fiber, and wood pieces must be tied, pinned, backed, or set into a real construction system. No floating plates, impossible molded bone armor, perfect one-piece shell armor, hollow broken helmets, random jewelry density, or item parts ignoring gravity.

All four pairs must be recognizably the same class and tier, but faction identity must be obvious through material logic, silhouette, palette, weapon shape, belt construction, head/neck motif, and item-base family. Final target:
Four separate paired male/female {TIER_NAME} {CLASS} images for a dark low-fantasy ARPG: crisp, faction-distinct, class-readable, tier-readable, construction-plausible, and useful as source concepts for later item extraction.

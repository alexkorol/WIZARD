# Jewelry and relic release notes

## Portfolio

- Exactly 50 rows arranged as ten complete five-rung ladders: three ring ladders, three amulet ladders, and four relic/curio ladders.
- Rings and amulets use the canonical `1x1` footprint. Relics use `1x1`, `2x1`, `2x2`, or `1x3` according to the concrete object silhouette.
- Every rung matches its tier. Adjacent rungs change macro silhouette or mechanical thesis, not only material.

## Reskin cleanup

- The former copper, bronze, and meteoric sheet-amulet repetition is now one broad sheet pendant, one conical bell amulet, and one miniature axe amulet in separate ladders.
- Repeated T5 ewer, bell, and censer concepts were replaced by a rod tripod, tall lampstand, deeply modeled long-spout vessel, and slide-key.
- The former jade pyxis repeat became a long rhomboid grinding palette.
- Repeated archaeological families that remain are non-adjacent and mechanically distinct at 48 px, such as a compact shoulder-pierced ampulla versus a handled three-foot ewer.

## Supply disposition

- All six canonical promoted rings and all four canonical promoted amulets are mapped exactly once by filename and SHA-256.
- Three promoted rings are direct row candidates; three are alternate aliases of the same bounded bronze bases.
- One promoted amulet is a direct row candidate. The other three remain `needs_user`: one is a multi-object tooth-and-cowrie cluster, one has an unsupported stone-cradle interface, and one relies on a tiny toggle fastening.
- All relevant ring and amulet review candidates are recorded once as `needs_user`; none is silently promoted.
- The three unresolved numeric locators from the candidate draft were removed. No production row depends on an index-only source.
- Existing balanced ritual outputs were not claimed here because the matching censer, pronged focus, and rhyton files are already assigned to the offhand/focus portfolio.

## Historical and generation policy

- Structural anchors remain Paleolithic through Late Antique and do not exceed the AD 600 ceiling.
- Player-facing forms suppress copied sacred imagery, readable script, solar/radial motifs, shrine miniatures, generic crystals, and modern or medieval hardware.
- No images were generated. Pixel variants remain downstream of user approval and sorting.

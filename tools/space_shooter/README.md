# Wireframe Space Shooter

A 3D wireframe space shooter game inspired by the classic Elite from the 1980s. Engage in space combat with retro wireframe graphics, a radar system, and various enemy types.

## Features

- **3D Wireframe Graphics**: Retro-style wireframe visuals
- **Space Combat**: Engage in dogfights with enemy ships
- **Radar System**: Track enemies and navigate space
- **Power-ups**: Collect power-ups to enhance your ship
- **Score System**: Earn points by destroying enemies
- **Shields**: Protect your ship with shields

## Controls

- **W/S**: Pitch up/down
- **A/D**: Roll left/right
- **Q/E**: Yaw left/right
- **Arrow Keys**: Move forward/backward/left/right
- **Space**: Fire lasers
- **R**: Restart game after game over

## How to Play

1. **Start the Game**: Open `index.html` in your web browser.
2. **Control Your Ship**: Use the controls to maneuver your ship and engage enemies.
3. **Track Enemies**: Use the radar to locate enemy ships.
4. **Fire Lasers**: Press the spacebar to fire lasers at enemies.
5. **Collect Power-ups**: Enhance your ship by collecting power-ups.
6. **Monitor Shields**: Keep an eye on your shields to avoid getting destroyed.
7. **Score Points**: Destroy enemies to earn points and achieve a high score.
8. **Game Over**: If your shields reach 0%, the game is over. Press 'R' to restart.
